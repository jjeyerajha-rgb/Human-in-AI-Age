# Weekly Report Prompt — "Human in the AI Age"

This file is the standing instruction set for the recurring **Monday** automation that
produces a new weekly research brief. Each run should produce one report under
`reports/` and update `reports/INDEX.md`.

## Goal

Pull thoughtful, current research and advice from the world's best practitioners —
**AI subject-matter experts, neuroscientists, behavioural scientists, and
academics** — on the key best practices that keep humans **alive, sane, and
cognitively active** as AI tools are increasingly adopted for everyday tasks at work
and in personal life.

The brief should help a thoughtful reader answer one question every week:
**"How do I get the benefits of AI without quietly outsourcing my own thinking?"**

## Each run, do this

1. **Research (don't recycle).** Search for material from roughly the **last 1–4
   weeks** plus any landmark studies still shaping the conversation. Prioritise:
   - Peer-reviewed studies and reputable preprints (note peer-review status).
   - Named experts: neuroscientists, cognitive/behavioural scientists, AI researchers,
     and academics — quote what they *actually recommend*.
   - Practical frameworks people can apply (e.g. the 3R principle, cognitive-fitness
     routines, "think first, prompt second").
2. **Stay balanced.** Include credible **counter-evidence and critiques** (e.g.
   methodological limits, "different ≠ worse" arguments). Do not overstate findings.
   Flag preprints and self-report studies as such.
3. **Be specific and citable.** Every claim should trace to a named source with a
   link. Prefer primary sources over hot takes.
4. **Make it actionable.** End with a concrete, do-this-now checklist for work and
   personal life.
5. **Write the report** to `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-human-in-the-ai-age.md` using
   `reports/TEMPLATE.md`. Use the Monday date of the current week.
6. **Update `reports/INDEX.md`** with a one-line entry linking the new report.
7. **Commit, push, and open a PR.**

## Tone

Calm, evidence-led, non-alarmist, and useful. We are pro-AI *and* pro-human: the goal
is skillful, intentional use — not abstinence and not surrender.

## Quality bar

- 6–10 distinct, named expert/study sources, each with a working link.
- At least one explicitly skeptical / counter-view source.
- A clear "what changed this week" angle so reports don't read as interchangeable.
- No fabricated quotes, numbers, or citations. If unsure, say so.
