# Human in the AI Age — Weekly Research Brief

A weekly research automation that curates thoughtful guidance from leading AI
practitioners, neuroscientists, behavioural scientists, and academicians on how
to **stay alive, sane, and cognitively active** while AI tools are increasingly
woven into our work and personal lives.

The goal is to keep the human in the loop: brain engaged, agency intact,
judgement sharp.

## How it works

- A Cursor Cloud Agent automation runs **every Monday at 03:30 UTC** (cron:
  `30 3 * * 1`).
- Each run pulls recent peer-reviewed papers, expert essays, lab reports, and
  practitioner commentary on AI's effects on human cognition, wellbeing, and
  relationships.
- The agent writes a new dated brief into [`reports/`](reports/) using
  [`TEMPLATE.md`](TEMPLATE.md) and commits it to this repository.
- File naming: `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-human-in-the-ai-age.md` (date = the Monday
  the report was generated).

## What each brief contains

1. **Executive summary** — the week's headline takeaways.
2. **Voices from AI practitioners** — what the people building AI are saying.
3. **Voices from neuroscientists & cognitive scientists** — what is happening
   inside the brain.
4. **Voices from behavioural scientists & academicians** — how to preserve
   agency at work and in society.
5. **Voices on personal life & social health** — relationships, loneliness,
   children, attention.
6. **Practices for the coming week** — concrete habits to try, for work and at
   home.
7. **Sources** — full citations.

## Editorial principles

- **Cite named experts and primary sources** (papers, lab pages, conference
  talks), not aggregator hot-takes.
- **Mix the camps**: AI builders, brain scientists, behavioural / social
  scientists, and humanities academicians.
- **Be honest about uncertainty**: flag preprints, sample sizes, and
  contested claims.
- **End with action**: every brief must produce 5–10 practices a reader can
  adopt this week.
